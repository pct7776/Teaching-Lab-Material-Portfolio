## 📰 2. Research Paper: The Role of Artificial Intelligence in Marketing and Advertising
*   **Author:** Poornachandra Tejaswi (Department of Information Science & Engineering, New Horizon College of Engineering)
*   **Keywords:** Personalized Marketing, Predictive Analytics, Conversational Commerce, Ethical AI, Customer Engagement

### 📑 Abstract
Artificial Intelligence (AI) has emerged as a transformative force across industries, and marketing and advertising are no exceptions. The integration of AI technologies such as machine learning, natural language processing, predictive analytics, and computer vision is reshaping how brands understand, interact with, and serve their customers. AI enables the automation of data analysis, the delivery of hyper-personalized experiences, and the optimization of marketing campaigns in real time. 

This paper explores the various AI technologies used in marketing, their practical applications, and their ability to enhance customer engagement, improve operational efficiency, and increase return on investment (ROI). Real-world case studies, such as those of Netflix and Sephora, illustrate the tangible benefits AI offers in creating more customized and impactful consumer experiences. However, the adoption of AI also raises significant challenges, including concerns about data privacy, algorithmic bias, transparency, and ethical responsibility. This study highlights both the immense opportunities and critical risks associated with AI-driven marketing strategies.

### 🛠️ Key Technical Modules Investigated
*   **Machine Learning (Supervised/Unsupervised/Reinforcement):** Algorithmic customer segmentation, churn forecasting vectors, dynamic pricing engines, and marketing attribution modeling.
*   **Natural Language Processing (NLP):** Sentiment parsing models, context-aware chatbot engineering, and localized voice search optimization metrics.
*   **Predictive Analytics:** Structuring predictive customer lifetime value (CLV) equations and lead scoring models.
*   **Computer Vision Frameworks:** Augmented Reality (AR) spatial configurations, visual search systems, and real-time facial analytics.
